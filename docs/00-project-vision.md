# Project Vision

Last updated: 2026-06-09

## Product Thesis

TalentBay should help candidates and companies make international hiring decisions with less uncertainty.

The first practical product is a Web2 SaaS/job board where a candidate can:

1. Open a vacancy.
2. See salary and target location.
3. Estimate net income after relocation costs, taxes, rent, insurance, and monthly expenses.
4. Register a profile.
5. Apply to the vacancy.

The main differentiator is not just "international vacancies". The differentiator is a transparent relocation and disposable-income calculation attached to vacancies.

## First Users

Primary first user:

- Candidate who is considering relocation or remote international work.

Secondary user:

- Company that wants candidates who understand relocation conditions and compensation reality.

Investor demo is important, but it should demonstrate a real product workflow rather than a static presentation only.

## Value Proposition

For candidates:

- Compare vacancies by real expected outcome, not only gross salary.
- Understand cost of relocation before applying.
- Reduce uncertainty around cities, rent, taxes, and living costs.

For companies:

- Publish vacancies for international candidates.
- Attract candidates who understand relocation tradeoffs.
- Reduce wasted conversations caused by unrealistic compensation expectations.

For investors/early supporters:

- See a working product foundation before any Web3/token layer is introduced.

## Current Positioning

The first MVP should be described as:

- SaaS platform for international recruitment.
- Job board with relocation calculator.
- Product demo for candidates, companies, and investors.

Avoid positioning the first MVP as:

- Token platform.
- Payment or custody provider.
- Guaranteed employment intermediary.
- KYC/AML platform.
- Smart-contract marketplace.

## Strategic Direction

Short term:

- Build a usable Web2 MVP.
- Prove that vacancy plus relocation calculation is useful.
- Prepare investor demo.

Mid term:

- Improve data quality and city coverage.
- Add company workflows.
- Add matching/recommendations if the core flow works.

Long term:

- Consider Web3/token features only after legal, compliance, and product-market validation.
